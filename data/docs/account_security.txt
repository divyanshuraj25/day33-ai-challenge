NimbusDesk Account and Security Guide

Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include one number. Passwords expire every 120 days. The last 6 passwords cannot be reused.

Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for workspace admins and optional for agents. NimbusDesk supports authenticator apps and hardware keys. SMS codes are not supported.

Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 20 minutes. Admins can unlock an account immediately from Settings > Members.

Data retention
Closed tickets are kept for 400 days and then permanently deleted. Enterprise workspaces can extend retention up to 7 years.
